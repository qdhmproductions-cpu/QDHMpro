Get to know your artist

  • My favorite part of performing is getting to meet and work with different collaborators and audiences.
  • When I was ten years old Britney Spears said, "There's only two types of people in the world. The ones that entertain and the ones that observe. Well, baby, I'm a put-on-a-show kinda girl" and I've known who I was and what I wanted to do ever since.
  • My favorite characters to play are mermaids or sirens, I also enjoy anything pirate themed!
  • My favorite type of performances would be fire aerials using my Lyra with wicks.
  • My first dance classes were tap dance when I was 5.
  • My parents love music and festivals, I discovered my love of flow arts (especially hula hoops) by going to festivals as a kid.
  • Growing up around festivals also lead to me choosing to not drink. My sobriety has helped me thrive in the entertainment industry whether it be working hospitality, nightlife, or concerts and festivals.
